Privacy Policy for Grimoire

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Grimoire: Private Practice mobile application ("Grimoire", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Grimoire is developed and operated by Douglas Davis ("the developer").

Grimoire is a private, personal journal for recording your own spiritual practice: reflections, spell and ritual work, grounding, tarot pulls and moon phases.

Summary

Grimoire does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information. Everything you write or add, including your pages, photos, tarot pulls, elements and settings, is stored only on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and we cannot see your Grimoire.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. There is no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What you create stays on your device, including:

  • Journal pages (reflections, spell work, ritual work, grounding) and their tarot pulls, moon phase and element
  • Photos you take or choose for a page
  • Your chosen element, hemisphere and other settings
  • Study notes, reading progress in the Athenaeum, your own rites, sigils you draw, and your progress through the Foundation
  • Your approximate location and birth details, if you choose to add them (see below)

Your Incantation

The phrase you set to open the app is stored in your device's secure Keychain. It never leaves your device and is never included in a backup. If you use Face ID or Touch ID to open the app, that check is performed entirely by your device; the app never receives your biometric data.

Location

Location is optional. If you choose to share it, the app asks once, at reduced (city-level) accuracy, and rounds it to about 10 km. You can type coordinates in by hand instead. It is used only on your device to work out sunrise, sunset and the planetary hours, and it is never sent anywhere.

Birth Details (Natal Sky)

Adding your birth date, time and place is optional. If you add them, they are stored only on your device, in its secure Keychain, and in your encrypted backup if you make one. The chart is calculated entirely on your device; your birth details are never sent anywhere. You can remove them at any time in Settings.

Backups

You can export a backup of your Grimoire. The backup file is encrypted with a password you choose and is saved only where you decide to put it (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). We never receive it, and without your password it cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

Photos

Photos you add are copied into the app's own private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. By default, photos taken with the in-app camera are not saved to your Photos library; you can choose to turn this on in Settings, in which case a copy is also saved to your Photos library, which is not protected by the app's lock.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example, before your free trial ends). These are created and delivered by your device; nothing is sent to us.

Network Use

Grimoire uses an internet connection only to process optional subscriptions through the Apple App Store and to display these legal pages. The books, plates and all astronomical calculations are built into the app and work offline. Your pages, photos, location, birth details and settings are never transmitted.

Free Trial and Subscriptions

Grimoire is free to download, with 14 free days of full use; no payment details are needed for the trial. The trial start date is stored on your device only. Optional subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for a page.
  • Photo Library: only through the system photo picker when you choose photos, and "add only" access if you turn on saving camera photos.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: optional, to open your Grimoire quickly.
  • Notifications: optional, for reminders.
  • Location: optional, once, approximate only, for sunrise, sunset and planetary hours.

Third Parties

We do not share data with any third parties. The only third party involved is Apple, solely for optional subscription payments.

Children

Grimoire is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not collect information from anyone, including children.

Data Security

Your Grimoire is protected by your incantation, your device's own security and encryption, and, for backups, by the backup password you choose. Keep your device, incantation and backup password safe.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions, contact the developer at douglas.davis3@icloud.com.
