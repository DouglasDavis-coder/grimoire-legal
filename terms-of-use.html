Nature of the App

Grimoire is a personal journal and reference for your own practice. It records what you do; it does not perform, cast or guarantee any spell, ritual or outcome, and it does not communicate with spirits or predict the future. Nothing in the app is medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. Use your own judgement, and seek professional help where appropriate.

Historic Texts

The Athenaeum contains historic texts, some centuries old. They reflect the beliefs, knowledge and attitudes of their own times, which may be inaccurate, outdated or offensive today, and some describe practices (such as animal sacrifice) that are recorded as history, not as instruction. Herbal texts are included for their astrological and historical content only: their medical claims are outdated, many plants are poisonous, and nothing in them should be eaten, drunk or applied.

Safety
